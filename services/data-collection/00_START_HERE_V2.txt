  🎉 NEW FEATURE: AUTO QR CODE NOTIFICATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NO MORE MANUAL LINKS! 
Just scan a QR code with your phone camera!


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🚀 QUICK START (4 EASY STEPS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Start the servers
──────────────────────────
    cd data_collection_agent
    ./start_servers.sh

STEP 2: Request data
──────────────────────────
    Open: http://localhost:8001
    Fill in phone number: +237670123456
    Select provider: MTN or ORANGE
    Click: "Request Data Collection"

STEP 3: Open QR notification
──────────────────────────
    A file is created at: /tmp/cub_consent_XXXXXXXX.html
    Open it in your browser:
        firefox /tmp/cub_consent_*.html

STEP 4: Scan with your phone
──────────────────────────
    📸 Open your phone camera
    📱 Point at the QR code on your screen
    🔔 Tap the notification
    ✅ Click "Approve"
    
    DONE! Data appears automatically! 🎉


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🎯 HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Computer: Request Data
    ↓
QR Code Created Automatically
    ↓
Open QR Notification in Browser
    ↓
Phone Camera Scans QR Code
    ↓
Consent Page Opens on Phone
    ↓
Tap "Approve"
    ↓
Data Collected & Displayed! ✅
