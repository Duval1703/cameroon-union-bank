STEP 1: Install ntfy app (1 min)
──────────────────────────────────
  📱 Play Store or App Store
  🔍 Search: "ntfy"
  📥 Install: "ntfy" by Binwiederhier
  ✅ Open the app


STEP 2: Subscribe to your topic (30 sec)
──────────────────────────────────
  In the ntfy app:
  1. Tap "+" button (bottom right)
  2. Enter topic name: cub-consent-allan
     (or use your own name: cub-consent-yourname)
  3. Tap "Subscribe"
  ✅ Done!


STEP 3: Test it NOW! (30 sec)
──────────────────────────────────
  On your computer:
  
  curl -d "Test from CUB!" ntfy.sh/cub-consent-allan
  
  (Replace cub-consent-allan with YOUR topic name)
  
  📱 CHECK YOUR PHONE!
  Did you get the notification? ✅ = IT WORKS!


STEP 4: Configure CUB (30 sec)
──────────────────────────────────
  cd data_collection_agent
  
  # Run the setup helper
  ./setup_ntfy.sh
  
  Or manually edit ntfy_config.json:
  {
    "topic_name": "cub-consent-allan",
    "enabled": true
  }


STEP 5: Restart servers (30 sec)
──────────────────────────────────
  cd data_collection_agent
  pkill -f python
  ./start_servers.sh
  
  Look for: "✅ Ntfy.sh notifications ENABLED"


STEP 6: Test the full flow! (10 sec)
──────────────────────────────────
  1. Open: http://localhost:8001
  2. Request data (phone: +237670123456, provider: MTN)
  3. Click "Request Data Collection"
  4. CHECK YOUR PHONE! 📱
  5. Tap "✅ Approve"
  6. Dashboard shows 120 transactions!
  
  ✅ DONE! 🎉
